The `ritmo-pixel-art` skill has four new principles from two blind tests: name each kind of piece by one exaggerated feature, starting from what it does in the game; give a solid three tones (top and two sides) chosen per piece, and make a line between parts a choice; draw details on a slanted face flat and lay them on it along the view's slope; and judge a set side by side and in pure black.
