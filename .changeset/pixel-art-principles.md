`ritmo-pixel-art` skill: the principles that make a game's pixel art read as one game, before the tool. One grid (the game's size in art pixels from the smallest detail, one pixel size, fitting the screen by a whole number, fractions kept in the simulation and rounded at the draw, text on the grid or on one layer), readability (what the player must notice is read first, gameplay signals in levels, silhouettes), one style (the characters set the rules, one palette of hue-shifted ramps, tiles that hide the grid unless the grid is the game, the pixel craft, one light), motion, and judging (in the game at display size on the target screen, references as named qualities, exceptions as written decisions). The `ritmo-tools pixel` part is unchanged under "The tool", and the `ritmo` skill's `ui-and-render` playbook points at the grid principles for camera, scaling, parallax, and text.
