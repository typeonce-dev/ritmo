The `ritmo-pixel-art` skill covers the new `ritmo-tools pixel` commands: when to bake a building, vehicle, or prop from a 3D model with `pixel bake` (and when to draw a sprite source instead), how to make and check a normal map with `pixel normals` for a game that lights its sprites, and how to read `pixel notes` now that a sheet's animations and their maps are reviewed apart (`parts`, `about: "map"`, `goneParts`).
