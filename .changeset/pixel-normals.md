`ritmo-tools pixel normals` makes a normal map for art a game lights: `.normal.png`, `.normals.json` (settings and corrections), and `.lit.png`. A sheet keeps each surface's direction through its animation, and the report measures what still flickers. `pixel review` shows art under a light you can drag, the whole set under one light, and comments can carry a direction; approvals cover the map. `pixel lint` checks a map against its art, and says when the art changed since. The method follows Pixel Art Lighter by Red Hat Sprite (MIT).
